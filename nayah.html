A letter from Yza

Dear Nayah,

Thank you for being my friend. I know I probably don’t say it enough, but I really want you to know how much I appreciate having you in my life.

Sometimes our friendship feels so normal that I forget to stop and think about how special it actually is. I appreciate every conversation we’ve had, every random topic we’ve talked about, every joke we’ve laughed at, every stupid thing we’ve done, and every moment we’ve spent together. Even the times when we were doing absolutely nothing somehow became memories worth keeping.

I think those simple moments are some of the most meaningful ones. We might not realize it while they’re happening, but someday, I know we’ll look back and remember all the little things that made our friendship what it is.

One thing I really love about our friendship is that we don’t always have to be serious. We can start with a normal conversation and somehow end up talking about the most random things ever. We can laugh at jokes that probably wouldn’t make sense to anyone else, and sometimes we understand each other without even having to explain anything.

With you, I feel like I can just be myself. I don’t have to pretend to be perfect, mature, or serious all the time. Sometimes we can just be two idiots laughing over something completely stupid, and honestly, those are some of my favorite moments.

Thank you for accepting me for who I am, including my weirdness, randomness, mood changes, overthinking, dramatic moments, endless stories, and all the times I probably talked way too much. I know I can be annoying sometimes, stubborn, or overly dramatic, but you still choose to be my friend, and that means more to me than I can explain.

Thank you for listening when I need to rant, even when my stories are unnecessarily long. Thank you for letting me tell you random things, even when they probably don’t matter that much. Thank you for putting up with me during my difficult moments. Most importantly, thank you for making me feel comfortable enough to be myself without constantly worrying about being judged.

I want you to know that you can always be yourself around me too. You don’t have to pretend that everything is okay when it isn’t. If you’re having a bad day, you can tell me. If you’re angry, sad, excited, confused, or just feeling strange for no reason, you can tell me. Even if you have a random story that you think nobody would care about, tell me anyway. I’ll listen.

You don’t always need to have the perfect words. Sometimes you can simply tell me that you’re not okay, and that’s enough. You don’t have to explain everything right away. I’ll be here to listen whenever you’re ready.

I’m really grateful for every memory we’ve made and every moment we’ve shared. I hope our friendship continues to grow, even as things around us change. There will probably be more random conversations, more stupid jokes, more arguments, more laughter, and more unforgettable moments, and I’m glad I get to experience them with you.

Thank you for being you, Nayah. Thank you for being my friend. I may not always say it, but I truly appreciate you more than you know.

I’m really lucky to have a friend like you.

Your friend, Yza
